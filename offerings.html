Offerings

Written By: Zachary Kai » Published: 21 Jul 2025 | Updated: 31 Jul 2026

Welcome! I offer various things, from services, written works, and other accoturements. Have a browse, and let me know if you have questions!

  • Beta Reading: thoughtful, detailed feedback on your manuscript.
  • Blog & Newsletter Setup: get your blog, newsletter, or both up and running!
  • Editing: fiction + non-fiction editing with eight years of experience.
  • Ghostwriting: your story, your voice. I do the writing.
  • Sensitivity Reading: thoughtful feedback on your written works.
  • Transcriptions: audio, video, and handwriting turned into clean, accurate text.
  • Websites: delightful and snappy personal sites built with static HTML.
  • Workshops: engaging creative sessions for groups, libraries, and schools.
  • Zine-Making: custom digital and handmade zines for your projects.
