Submission Guidelines

To be included in the Oceania Web Atlas:

  • You must be from or living in Oceania
  • It must be your personal site, blog, portfolio, or store
  • The site should reflect something about yourself

Join The Directory

Want your site to be featured in the Oceania Web Atlas? I'd love to hear from you! Use the form below or email me: hi@zacharykai.net. (You can also email me for removals or changes.)

I review submissions on Mondays and Thursdays. I’ll get to yours on the next review day!
