Submission Guidelines

To be included in the Crosswinds + Currents Directory:

  • You must be from or living in the Asia/Pacific region
  • It must be your personal site, blog, portfolio, or store
  • The site should reflect something about yourself

Join The Directory

Want your site to be featured in Crosswinds + Currents? I'd love to hear from you! Use the form below or email me: hi@zacharykai.net. (You can also email me for removals or changes.)

I review submissions on Mondays and Thursdays. I’ll get to yours on the next review day!
