Sites

  • Varve: original writing, fascinating thoughts, and clever nooks and crannies.
  • Wing Pang: : currently under re-construction, but clever usage of graph paper!

Submission Guidelines

To be included in the Static HTML Sites directory:

  • Your site must use static HTML + CSS (some scripting for extra functionality is fine!)
  • It must be your personal site or blog.
  • The site should reflect something about yourself.

Join The Directory

Want your site to be featured in the Static HTML Sites directory? I'd love to hear from you! Use the form below or contact me. (Email me for removals or changes.)

I review submissions on Mondays and Thursdays. I’ll get to yours on the next review day!
