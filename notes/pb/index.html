People And Blogs

Run By: Zachary Kai » Founded By: Manuel Moreale » Published: 1 Jun 2026 | Updated: 7 Aug 2026

People And Blogs is a weekly series where interesting people talk about themselves and their blogs. It celebrates the humans behind personal sites.

The series was founded and lovingly run for years by Manuel Moreale. I've since taken it over, and it's now my honour to carry it forward: same spirit, same weekly rhythm, same belief in the open, personal web.

Huge thanks to Manu for trusting me with it.

If you or someone you know would like to be interviewed, contact me.
